Microeconomics, made interactive

Five sessions built around graphs you can grab and move. Drag curves, change prices, and watch surplus, profit and deadweight loss update live — the fastest way to see why the diagrams look the way they do.

Session 1

Economic Thinking & Consumer Choice

Scarcity, opportunity cost and how a consumer picks the best bundle. Drag a bundle around the budget line; see income & substitution effects.

Budget line · Indifference curves · Marginal thinking Session 2

Demand, Supply, Equilibrium & Elasticity

Build curves from willingness to pay, drag them to shift, and see consumer/producer surplus, price controls, taxes and elasticity.

WTP · Surplus · Deadweight loss · Elasticity Session 3

Firms, Costs & Perfect Competition

Cost curves, MR = MC, the shutdown rule, and how free entry pushes profit to zero.

MC · ATC · Profit · Price-taking Session 4

Market Power & Market Structures

Monopoly, oligopoly, monopolistic competition, price discrimination and monopsony — compare on one graph.

Monopoly · Cournot · Price discrimination · Monopsony Session 5

Game Theory & Externalities

Solve any 2×2 payoff matrix, play a repeated prisoner’s dilemma, and fix externalities with Pigouvian taxes and subsidies.

Nash equilibrium · Collusion · Externalities
